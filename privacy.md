# Skippy Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what information Skippy, a Discord application (the "Bot"), collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using the Bot, you agree to this Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Bot only collects the data necessary to provide its features. This may include:

- **Discord identifiers** – user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs.
- **Basic profile data** – usernames, display names, and avatars as provided by the Discord API.
- **Configuration data** – settings that server administrators or users choose to save in the Bot.
- **Command and interaction data** – the commands you run and the inputs you provide to them.
- **Message content** – only where required for a specific feature you use, and only as permitted by Discord.

We do not collect passwords, email addresses, payment information, or any data Discord does not make available to applications.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to:

- Operate, maintain, and provide the Bot's features.
- Store your preferences and server configuration.
- Diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and improve the Bot.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties, except:

- With service providers that host or operate the Bot (e.g., hosting providers), solely to run the Bot.
- When required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the Bot.

## 4. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as needed to provide the Bot's features. Data associated with a server may be deleted when the Bot is removed from that server. Logs used for debugging are kept for a limited period and then deleted.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

You may request access to, or deletion of, data the Bot has stored about you by contacting us (see below). Server administrators can remove the Bot at any time to stop further data collection in their server. Depending on where you live (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights, which we will honor as required by law.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country to use Discord), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## 8. Discord

The Bot operates on Discord, which collects and processes data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). We are not responsible for Discord's practices.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes. Continued use of the Bot after changes means you accept the revised Policy.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, open an issue at [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/theadlee/discord-legal/issues).
